Explore the Data: Interactive Player Explorer

Interactive Player Explorer

Use the interactive visualization below to investigate how individual player performance varies with age across Europe’s top five domestic soccer leagues during the 2025–2026 season.

How to Use This Graphic

  • Filter by League & Position: Use the dropdown filters below to narrow the players shown. You can choose one or more leagues and positions, or leave them empty to view all players.
  • Hover for Details: Move your cursor over any marker to see the player’s name, age, squad, league, position, minutes played, goals, assists, and goal contributions per 90.
  • Zoom & Pan: Click and drag on any region of the plot to zoom in. Use the top-right Plotly toolbar to pan, or double-click anywhere on the plot area to reset the zoom.

Data Source

Data was retrieved from Kaggle: Football Players Statistics (2025–2026) by Hubert Sidorowicz, sourced from FBref.
